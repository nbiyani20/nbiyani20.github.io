About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Northeastern University, Boston, where I conduct research in the Energy Efficient Circuits and Systems Group. My research focuses on ultra-low-power analog and mixed-signal integrated circuit design, analog computing hardware, RF energy harvesting, power management integrated circuits, and biomedical integrated systems.


My work spans the complete custom IC design flow, from system architecture and behavioral modeling to transistor-level design, simulation, custom analog layout, parasitic extraction, post-layout verification, silicon validation, and laboratory characterization.


During my Ph.D., I have designed analog and RF building blocks including low-dropout regulators, bandgap voltage references, switched-capacitor DC-DC converters, RF rectifiers, low-noise amplifiers, mixers, voltage-controlled oscillators, operational amplifiers, comparators, Gm-C filters, and analog computing circuits.
