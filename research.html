Research

My research focuses on ultra-low-power analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits, energy-efficient computing, RF energy harvesting, biomedical electronics, and power-management systems.

Ultra-Low-Power Analog Computing

I develop analog computing circuits and system-level architectures for energy-efficient machine-learning applications. My work includes current-mode distance computation, analog multipliers, and ultra-low-power computing blocks for classification and sensing systems.

Biomedical Integrated Circuits

I work on ultra-low-power biomedical SoCs for applications such as seizure detection. This research combines analog front ends, filtering, signal-strength processing, analog computation, and system-level validation.

One of my research efforts demonstrated an analog computing machine-learning SoC achieving 265 nW/channel power consumption, 99.1% sensitivity, and strong robustness across temperature variation.

RF Energy Harvesting

My RF energy-harvesting research focuses on efficient RF-to-DC conversion for energy-constrained and battery-free systems. This work includes CMOS rectifier design, ultra-low-power comparators, and circuit optimization for improved conversion efficiency.

RF Front-End Circuits

I design low-power RF building blocks including low-noise amplifiers, mixers, and voltage-controlled oscillators. My work focuses on reducing power consumption while maintaining competitive linearity, gain, and robustness across operating conditions.

Power Management Integrated Circuits

My power-management work includes low-dropout regulators, bandgap references, hysteresis comparators, and switched-capacitor DC-DC converters for on-chip voltage regulation.

I explore architectures that provide stable operation across temperature, process, supply, and load variations while minimizing power consumption.

Low-Power Analog Filtering

I have designed low-power Gm-C filters for wireless and biomedical receiver applications, including fourth-order biquad filter architectures optimized for low noise and energy efficiency.
