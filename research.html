Technical Expertise

Analog & Mixed-Signal IC Design

Operational amplifiers, OTAs, comparators, hysteresis comparators, differential amplifiers, precision current mirrors, track-and-hold/sample-and-hold circuits, SAR ADCs, precision bias circuits, Gm-C filters, analog multipliers, and CMOS analog design.

RF & Wireless Circuits

Low-noise amplifiers, mixers, voltage-controlled oscillators, RF rectifiers, RF energy harvesting circuits, impedance matching, and low-power RF front-end design.

Power Management ICs

Low-dropout regulators, bandgap voltage references, switched-capacitor DC-DC converters, RF rectifiers, ultra-low-power voltage regulation, and energy harvesting systems.

Biomedical & Analog Computing

ReLU, seizure detection SoCs, analog signal processing, ultra-low-power biomedical systems, and current-mode analog computing architectures.

Verification & Physical Design

PVT verification, Monte Carlo mismatch analysis, statistical yield optimization, RC extraction (RCX), post-layout parasitic extraction (PEX), DRC/LVS, post-layout simulation, custom analog layout.

Electromagnetic Simulation & PCB Design

Multi-layer PCB design, signal integrity (SI), power integrity (PI), high-speed PCB design, EM simulation, RF co-simulation, transmission line design, package parasitic analysis.

Silicon Validation & Lab Characterization

RF characterization, S-parameter measurements, gain/frequency response and noise characterization, VNAs, spectrum analyzers, oscilloscopes, signal generators, SMUs, function generators, DMMs.

EDA Tools

Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre(ADE Explorer/Assembler), Cadence Layout Suite, Synopsys digital flow, MATLAB, Simulink, SPICE, Verilog/Verilog-A/SystemVerilog, Python, and TCL scripting.
