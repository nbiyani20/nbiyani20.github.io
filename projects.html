Selected Projects

My project work spans ultra-low-power analog and mixed-signal IC design, RF circuits, power management, biomedical systems, and analog computing.

Ultra-Low-Power Analog Computing SoC for Seizure Detection

Designed and validated a system-level biomedical SoC integrating an analog front end, filters, RSSI circuitry, and an analog multiplier for machine-learning-based seizure detection.

  • Achieved 265 nW/channel power consumption.
  • Achieved 99.1% sensitivity.
  • Demonstrated only 1.3% sensitivity variation across an 80°C temperature range.
  • Used Cadence Virtuoso, MATLAB, mixed-signal design techniques, and thermal-chamber testing.

RF-DC Rectifier for RF Energy Harvesting

Designed an RF rectifier and ultra-low-power comparator for efficient on-chip RF energy harvesting in TSMC 65-nm CMOS.

  • Performed schematic design, simulation, and optimization using Cadence tools.
  • Focused on ultra-low-power RF-to-DC conversion for energy-constrained systems.
  • Reduced power consumption significantly compared with prior implementations.

Ultra-Low-Power RF Front-End: LNA, Mixer, and VCO

Designed a low-power RF front end including a low-noise amplifier, mixer, and voltage-controlled oscillator in GPDK 45 nm.

  • Performed schematic design and PVT simulations.
  • Reduced power consumption while improving circuit linearity.
  • Used Cadence Virtuoso for RF circuit design and characterization.

Bandgap Reference and Switched-Capacitor DC-DC Converter

Designed an ultra-low-power bandgap reference and switched-capacitor converter for on-chip voltage regulation.

  • Achieved approximately 80% conversion efficiency across a 10× load range.
  • Achieved a stable approximately 1 V reference across a wide temperature range.
  • Performed temperature, load, and corner simulations.

Hysteresis Comparator and Low-Dropout Regulator

Designed an ultra-low-power hysteresis comparator and LDO for efficient on-chip voltage regulation.

  • Optimized the circuits for low quiescent power.
  • Performed schematic design, simulation, and optimization using Cadence Virtuoso.

Fourth-Order Gm-C Biquad Filter

Designed a fourth-order low-pass Gm-C filter for low-power wireless receiver applications, including ZigBee and WBAN.

  • Implemented cascaded OTA-based biquad stages with common-mode feedback.
  • Optimized for low power, low input-referred noise, and stability.
  • Demonstrated strong energy efficiency using a standardized figure of merit.

K-Means Clustering Distance Calculation Circuit

Developed an analog circuit architecture for implementing K-Means clustering distance calculations for machine-learning applications.

  • Used CMOS analog design, Python, and Cadence Virtuoso.
  • Focused on ultra-low-power current-mode computation.
  • Achieved substantial power reduction compared with previously published approaches.
